Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

agent.wei is a personal tool operated by one individual for the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

After the account owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, the app reads: basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar), profile details, account statistics (followers, likes, video count), and the owner's public video list with view, like, comment and share counts.

How we use it

We use this data only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's account and to show the owner's own performance analytics.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, share, or disclose TikTok data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites. After revocation, we delete stored tokens and cached data.
